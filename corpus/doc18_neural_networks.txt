Neural networks are machine-learning models inspired by layers of connected processing units. Each connection has a numeric weight, and each layer transforms its inputs into a new representation. During training, the model adjusts weights to reduce the difference between predictions and known answers.

Deep learning refers to neural networks with many hidden layers. Convolutional networks are effective for images, while transformer architectures are widely used for language tasks. Training requires data, a loss function, an optimization method, and careful evaluation on examples not used during learning.

Information retrieval can use neural models for semantic ranking, query understanding, and document representation. A traditional TF-IDF engine is easier to inspect and explain, while a neural ranker can learn more complex relationships. Practical systems often combine lexical retrieval for reliable candidate generation with learned ranking for final ordering.
